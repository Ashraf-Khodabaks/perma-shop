What does each layer do? The DWR finish helps water bead on the outer fabric. The PU layer provides the main waterproof barrier, while seam tape helps prevent water entering through stitched seams. Actual performance depends on the full garment construction and should be tested.

DWR means Durable Water Repellent: a durable water-repellent treatment applied to the outer fabric.

Which PFAS do we use?

Made without intentionally added PFAS

At Perma, we choose not to intentionally add PFAS to our clothing to make it repel water, oil or dirt. For the products covered by this commitment, that means no fluorinated DWR finish and no PTFE/ePTFE membrane.

An important caveat: “made without intentionally added PFAS” is more precise than an absolute claim that no measurable PFAS is present in any garment. Traces could, for example, enter through raw materials or manufacturing processes. That is why a strong claim should be supported by clear material specifications, supplier information and appropriate checks.

This is Perma’s proposed material standard. Final product claims should be supported by supplier documentation and, where appropriate, testing.
